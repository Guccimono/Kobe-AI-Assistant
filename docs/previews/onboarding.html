Welcome to Kobe

An assistant that lives in your notch.

Kobe shows your messages, calendar, money and code at the top of the screen, and you talk to it like a colleague. To do that it needs two things, and we set both up together in about five minutes.

1. An engine
Hermes Agent runs on your Mac and does the work: reads, remembers, acts. Free and open source. If it isn't here yet, Kobe installs it with you.
2. A brain
An AI model the engine thinks with. You pick one; the best ones are paid by use, one runs on your Mac for free.

Nothing leaves your Mac except what you send to the brain you choose. No account with us, no telemetry.

What Kobe can see on this Mac.

Each one asks macOS for permission the first time you open its page, not now. You can say no to any of them.

Mail
Unread count and the latest messages. Never sends.
Calendar
Today and the month. Opens events in Calendar.
Reminders
What's due; add one from the notch; tick when you click.
Notes
Recent notes; edit one in the notch.

The engine reads them through Kobe, on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded unless you ask Kobe something about it.
