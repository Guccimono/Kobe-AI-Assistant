Writing folds Now

The moment you type, Now shrinks to a 44 pt rail and the chat gets the full width. The field grows to ten lines instead of five. Sending keeps you in writing mode.

The rail keeps the count

Folded Now still shows how many things need you, in amber. The expand icon at its top, a click anywhere on the rail, ⌘\ or Esc brings the split back.

Home opens on what matters

When something needs you, Home opens split as today. When Now is empty, it opens writing first. "Now first" is the mirror, for reading the day.
